Two ways to monetise the gas

Electricity now, biomethane when the boiler matters more

Route A · Power & heat

Displace diesel and grid import

Many inland Sabah mills run diesel gensets at RM 0.55–0.75/kWh because the grid is weak or the connection is slow. POME biogas at 60% CH₄ can carry a large share of that load at a fraction of the fuel cost, with useful heat as a by-product.

  • Engine electrical output from measured or calculated gas flow
  • Heat recovery for boiler feedwater and process hot water
  • Island mode or parallel operation with existing diesel sets
Biogas to power →

Route B · Biomethane

Boiler fuel and bio-CNG

Where the mill needs boiler fuel more than electricity, POME biogas can be upgraded to ≥97% CH₄ and used in the existing burner, or compressed as bio-CNG and moved by trailer — a virtual pipeline to sites without a gas grid.

  • PSA, water scrubbing or membrane upgrading
  • Compression to 250 bar for tube-trailer delivery
  • Burner retrofit support and gas quality guarantees

Local presence

Sabah-based, with the statutory credentials that matter
