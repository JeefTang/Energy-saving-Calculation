About KSTS

A Sabah contractor with the right registrations — and a factory behind it

KSTS K. S. Technical Services is a CIDB-registered, Bumiputera-owned technical services company based in Telupid, Sabah. We deliver biogas and bioenergy projects for the palm oil and agro-industrial sector in Sabah and Sarawak, with process and equipment support from ZERO CARBON ASSISTANT in Shanghai.

The company

What KSTS does

Founded to serve the estates and mills of the Labuk–Sugut and Telupid belt, KSTS works across civil, mechanical and electrical scope, and holds the statutory registrations that a biogas plant needs before it can legally run.

  • Agricultural, building, civil and mechanical works
  • I.C.E. and steam engineering visitation services under FMA 1967
  • Energy management, project management and technical solutions
  • Land transportation services
  • Drone aerial surveying and agricultural spraying

Why it matters

Gas quality, engine warranty and civil works are usually three contracts with three excuses. Here they meet at one interface.

Why it matters

Local statutory knowledge

Why it matters

Factory-backed pricing

How we work

Four steps, no surprises

Step 1

Data

Throughput, pond volume, one to two weeks of gas flow and composition, tariff and load profile.

Step 2

Pre-feasibility

Gas balance, recommended capacity, indicative capex and opex, payback range. Free of charge.

Step 3

Engineering

P&ID, GA drawings, equipment selection, firm quotation, and the commercial model that fits.

Step 4

Delivery

Supply, install, commission, DOSH witness, operator training, then O&M if required.
