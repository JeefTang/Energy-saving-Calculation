Revenue assumes the plant is paid the stated value for every kWh generated. Capex excludes financing costs, land and any grid upgrade charged by the utility. No escalation or degradation applied — a screening model, not a bankable one.

Method & constants. Methane LHV 35.8 MJ/Nm³ = 9.94 kWh/Nm³. Biogas energy = flow × CH₄ fraction × 9.94. Electrical output = fuel energy × electrical efficiency. Recoverable heat = fuel energy × heat recovery fraction. 1 tonne of diesel-equivalent taken as 35.8 GJ. All results are indicative; equipment guarantees are issued only against measured site data.

Why we publish the formulas. Most supplier calculators hide the assumptions. Ours are visible because a biogas project is decided on gas quality and operating hours, not on a polished number.
