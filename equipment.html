Balance of plant

The parts between the pond and the engine

Capture

Pond cover & gas collection

HDPE or geomembrane cover, gas collection headers, condensate traps, pressure relief and inspection hatches. Retrofittable to existing covered lagoons.

Desulfurisation

Biological + iron oxide

Biological trickling filter for bulk H₂S removal, followed by iron-oxide or activated-carbon polishing vessels in duty/standby arrangement.

Conditioning

Chiller, blower, filters

Refrigerated dryer, coalescing filters, Roots blower with VFD, flame arrestor, gas train with shut-off and relief valves.

Storage

Gas holder

Membrane or double-membrane holder to buffer mill load swings and keep the engine off the trip line during upstream upsets.

Safety

Flare & gas detection

Enclosed or open flare sized for full gas flow, CH₄/H₂S detection, emergency shutdown logic and explosion-proof instrumentation in the gas area.

Heat

Heat recovery

Exhaust gas heat exchanger and jacket-water circuit producing hot water at 85–95 °C, or steam via an exhaust boiler where the mill needs it.
